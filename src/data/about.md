precip.science is a personal research project by [Ian C. Cornejo](https://iancornejo.github.io), a radar meteorologist in Fort Collins, Colorado. It runs established radar algorithms on NOAA's real-time radar mosaics, to see what they show across a whole country at once and where they need more work.

Everything here is experimental and unofficial. The products aren't forecasts or warnings, and they can be late, wrong or missing. They aren't official products of NOAA or the National Weather Service, and neither NOAA nor any of the teams credited below endorse them. For forecasts and warnings, go to the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov).

## How products are versioned

Each product carries a version (v0.1, v0.2, and so on). Every output records the code version and the parameters that made it, so any frame can be traced and remade.
